Welcome

Rakah Tracker counts your prayer for you, so you never lose track of which rak'ah you are in.

📱

Place your phone

Motion: keep the phone in your pocket, upright, the same way every time. Camera: lay it screen-up on your mat.

🤲

Start praying

Pick your prayer, tap Enable Motion, then stand still until the countdown ends. After that, just pray.

📳

What the buzzes mean

Two short buzzes: a sujud was counted. One long triple buzz: a mistake (missed or extra sujud, missing or false Tashahhud).

⏸️

Need to look at the screen?

Tap Pause before taking the phone out, and Resume when it is back in your pocket. The big number is your rak'ah; the small one counts the sujuds in it.
