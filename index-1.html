Once installed, the counter, the bead ring, the rak'ah/position display and manual counting all work fully offline — no signal needed. Voice detection needs a connection (it runs through your phone's built‑in speech recognition, sending short audio snippets to your browser vendor's servers). Camera detection runs entirely on your phone — frames are analyzed locally for brightness only; nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. This tool follows the standard 5‑takbir‑per‑rakah structure (with an extra takbir wherever you sit for tashahhud) — some schools of thought and witr/nafl prayers differ slightly, so treat it as a memory aid, not a ruling.
